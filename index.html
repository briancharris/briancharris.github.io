About BCCS

A solutions-first consulting firm built on a broad skillset and deep technical knowledge.

BCCS was founded on a simple premise: problems happen fast, delays are costly, and the quicker you can get a solution in place the quicker you can get back to business.

With a decade of experience spanning eCommerce operational best practices, 0-to-1 product launches, technical platform modernization, and enterprise SaaS integrations, we bridge the gap between deep technical execution and high-level business strategy.

Our Approach

Structured for speed, flexible by design.

We don't follow rigid frameworks. We adapt our process to the reality of your situation — but every engagement follows a consistent logical thread.

1

Diagnose

Rapid assessment of the current state — technology, team, process, and data. We identify the real bottleneck, not the assumed one.

2

Design

Build a clear, executable plan with defined outcomes. We prototype early to validate direction before committing resources.

3

Redesign

We get a fix deployed, and circle back and do it over again with feedback from all stakeholders. An imperfect fix can get you 80% of the way there in 20% of the time, but we still have to close out the last 20%.

4

Deliver

Hands-on execution with your team. We don't hand off a document and leave — we ship alongside you through sprint cycles.

5

Transfer

We build to hand off. Documentation, knowledge transfer, and team upskilling so your organization owns what we build — permanently.
